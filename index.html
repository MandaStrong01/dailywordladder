Daily Word Ladder


How to play
  1. Read the two words.Your ladder starts at the top word and ends at the dashed word at the bottom.
  2. Change one letter.Type a new word that differs from the one above it by exactly one letter. Press Enter to place it. hate→late
  3. Use real words.Every rung must be a common four-letter word. No names, and no repeating a word.
  4. Reach the bottom word.Stuck? Undo a rung, or take a hint. A hint tells you which letter to change, then the word.
  5. Beat the best route.Your step count is compared with the shortest possible ladder. Solve every day to build a streak and climb the leaderboard.
